About
HUSORO

Established in 2024, HUSORO Technologies is a deep-tech company dedicated to advancing the field of unmanned robotics. Our mission is to protect human lives and transform critical operations through intelligent, reliable, and purpose-driven robotic systems.

Built on a strong foundation of in-house research and development, HUSORO combines expertise in AI, autonomy, and advanced sensing to deliver technologies that are both robust and future-ready.

With a clear focus on innovation, precision, and real-world impact, HUSORO is shaping the next generation of robotic solutions where technology serves humanity with trust, adaptability, and purpose.

Product
M.I.R.A.G.E.

Minefield Intelligent Radar and Autonomous Ground Explorer

M.I.R.A.G.E. is an advanced military-grade UGV engineered for intelligent landmine detection and classification. It employs sensor fusion technology, combining data from multiple sensors and advanced radar systems to accurately identify buried threats in real time. Designed for reliability in challenging terrains, M.I.R.A.G.E enhances operational safety, precision, and efficiency in demining missions.

Technology Features

Real-Time Landmine Detection

Advanced radar systems detect buried explosive devices with high precision and accuracy.

Advanced Sensor Fusion Technology

Multiple sensor data combined for enhanced threat identification and reduced false positives.

Dual-Mode Operation: Autonomous & Manual

Flexible operation modes for different mission requirements and terrain conditions.
